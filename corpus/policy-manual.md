# Part 1 — Travel & Subsistence Allowances

## 1.1 Domestic Travel Allowances
- §1.1.1 Standard daily lodging allowance for domestic travel within metropolitan zones is capped at $150.00 per night.
- §1.1.2 Daily meal reimbursement rate is capped at $45.00 per day with itemized receipts required for any single expense over $15.00.
- §1.1.3 Private vehicle mileage rate for business travel is reimbursed at $0.58 per mile.

## 1.2 International Travel & Relocation
- §1.2.1 International flights exceeding 8 continuous hours qualify for Premium Economy booking upon prior manager approval.
- §1.2.2 Relocation disturbance allowance is granted as a one-time lump sum of $2,500.00 for relocations exceeding 50 miles.

# Part 2 — Medical & Wellness Benefits

## 2.1 Outpatient Claims
- §2.1.1 Annual preventative dental care reimbursement limit is $750.00 per employee per calendar year.
- §2.1.2 Vision and optical hardware benefits provide up to $300.00 every two calendar years for prescription lenses and frames.
- §2.1.3 Mental health therapy sessions are reimbursed at 80% of eligible cost up to a maximum of 12 sessions per calendar year.

# Part 3 — Equipment & Remote Work

## 3.1 Home Office Stipend
- §3.1.1 Initial remote work workstation setup stipend is $500.00 payable upon successful completion of the probationary period.
- §3.1.2 Monthly home broadband and mobile connectivity subsidy is $60.00 per full calendar month.

# Part 4 — Claims Submission & Dispute Timelines

## 4.3 Claim Deadlines & Appeals
- §4.3.1 All expense and reimbursement claims must be submitted within 60 calendar days of the event or invoice date.
- §4.3.2 Formal appeal for denied claims must be lodged within 30 calendar days following official notification of denial.
- §4.3.3 The review board shall issue a final written decision on appeals within 45 calendar days of receipt.

# Part 6 — Income & Earnings Disregards

## 6.4 Standard Allowances & Disregards
- §6.4.1(a) The standard weekly earnings disregard for single claimants without dependants is $200.00 per week.
- §6.4.1(b) The enhanced weekly earnings disregard for claimants with qualifying dependants or disability support needs is $350.00 per week.
- §6.4.2 In calculating net monthly assessable income, allowable employment deductions shall not exceed $120.00 per calendar month.
